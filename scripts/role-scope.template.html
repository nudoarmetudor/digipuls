What each role can actually see

Seven roles are defined. This is what each one reaches in the running code and the live database — not what the role name promises. Three were materially narrower than intended, and the largest group of pilot users was the worst served. Everything below has since been built and deployed; the findings are kept with their original wording so the record shows what was wrong, not only what is right now.

All nine findings closed, plus one regression they caused, recorded at the end. Deployed at {{commit}}; {{testsPass}} tests passing. Scope is decided in four places

Most of what follows was a disagreement between these layers. A capability can be held and still be unreachable; a page can be reachable and still be empty. Both were true.

The role's default capability set

ROLE_DEFAULTS plus the baseline every role holds (feedback.submit). This is what the account screen shows and what an administrator reasons about.

Per-post overrides

Only differences from the default are stored, so a role's defaults can be revised later without rewriting every account.

The route guard

requireCapability(...), and in one case requireRole('SCHOOL_TEAM') as well. Where the guard demands a role the capability model doesn't mention, a held capability becomes unusable.

The data filter inside the route

activeSchoolId(req) / activeTerritoryId(req) off the active post. This is the layer nobody sees in the admin UI, and where the emptiest pages came from.

Role by role

Counts are live posts in the pilot database. “Reaches” is what the guards admit; “gets” is what the page actually renders once the data filter has run.

Echipa digitală

SCHOOL_TEAM {{schoolTeamPosts}} posts · one per pilot school

Reaches

  • /school — the 19-indicator assessment, device inventory, network checklist
  • /school/history — levels over cycles
  • /school/cycles/:id/plan — the development plan
  • /feedback — its own tickets

Gets

  • Its own school only, enforced by cycle.schoolId on every route
  • Confirmed cycles locked; edits need a continuation cycle
  • Names its meta-mentor — the link was in the database all along, in the other direction
  • A post with no school explains the misconfiguration instead of landing on the feedback page

Works. The best-served role. Object-level scoping is correct and the assessment flow is complete.

Meta-mentor

META_MENTOR {{mentorPosts}} posts · the pilot's largest group

Reaches

  • /ministry — all {{allSchools}}
  • /ministry/compliance — Order 675 device and network monitor
  • /territorial — regional dashboard
  • /feedback — its own tickets

Was getting

  • Regional dashboard: zero schools. The post names no district, and { territoryId: null } means IS NULL — it matched schools with no district, of which there are none
  • The school they mentor was invisible. {{mentorsWithSchool}} of {{mentorPosts}} posts name a school; no route read it
  • Could not open a school's workspace — requireRole('SCHOOL_TEAM') blocked it
  • School detail showed the confirmed cycle only, so a school mid-assessment showed almost nothing

Fixed. A mentor now lands on the school they support, sees what is rated and what is blocking confirmation — drafts included — and their regional view resolves to that school's district.

MEC Task Force

MINISTRY {{ministryPosts}} post · national

Reaches

  • /ministry — national dashboard and CSV export
  • /ministry/compliance — Annex 2 quotas, Annex 5 checklist
  • /ministry/schools/:id — per-school detail

Gets

  • All {{allSchools}}; filters on district, enrolment band, status, compliance, domain minimums
  • Official record is the latest confirmed cycle, never a newer draft
  • Only districts that actually hold a school are offered as filters
  • The validation heading appears only when there is something under it

Fixed. The district filter is wired to the list the route was already loading and discarding.

Autoritate teritorială

TERRITORIAL {{territorialPosts}} post · {{territorialName}}, {{territorialSchools}}

Reaches

  • /territorial — schools in its own raion
  • /territorial/schools/:id — detail, refused outside the district
  • POST /territorial/schools/:id/flag — raise a concern

Gets

  • Correctly scoped by territoryId, on the list and the detail page
  • Concerns it raised, read back and shown on the school's page

Fixed. A flag used to go only to the audit log, which this role cannot read — it filed a concern and never saw it again. Concerns are now visible to the district that raised them and to the Ministry the escalation is aimed at.

Partener de finanțare

PARTNER {{partnerPosts}} post · UNICEF Moldova

Reaches

  • /partner — filterable overview, aggregate figures only

Gets

  • All {{allSchools}}; domain scores, compliance, enrolment band
  • No per-indicator evidence, by design — case-by-case reading, not matchmaking
  • Schools without a confirmed cycle read as not started, rather than as blank rows

Works as specified. The narrowest role, and deliberately so.

Partener strategic

STRATEGIC_PARTNER {{strategicPosts}} post · Clasa Viitorului

Reaches

  • /strategic — Domain C triage: C1 teacher competence, C3 mentoring capacity, C4 AI literacy

Gets

  • Schools ranked lowest-capacity-first, one disclosed sort, no hidden weighting
  • Ranked on the confirmed cycle, not on whatever draft happens to be open
  • Schools that cannot be ranked listed separately, not mixed in at the top

Fixed. Both mistakes were invisible on screen with the data of the day — one needed a school to open a second cycle, the other needed an unrated indicator. Moved into a service so tests pin them.

Administrator

ADMIN {{adminPosts}} posts · owner and fallback

Reaches

  • Every dashboard, plus /admin/users, /admin/schools/new, /admin/audit-log
  • /feedback/backlog — every ticket and its status

Gets

  • Full account management, split into admin.users and admin.grant so managing people is not the same as handing out powers
  • The picker marks view.school as needing a school-team post, so a box that changes nothing says so
  • Regional view resolves rather than showing an empty list

Fixed. An administrator held view.school and could never use it; the control is now honest about what it needs.

Findings, worst first

Original wording kept, so the record shows what was wrong.

Fixed · was high

The regional dashboard was empty for every post that held the capability

Prisma reads where: { territoryId: null } as IS NULL, not as “no filter”. Every meta-mentor and both administrators hold view.regional, saw the link in the sidebar, and landed on a list of nothing. No school in the pilot lacks a district, so the query could only ever return zero rows. {{regionalHolders}} posts hold it; all {{regionalResolving}} now resolve.

src/routes/territorial.js → schoolOverview.schoolsWithLatestCycle

Fixed · was high

A meta-mentor could not see the school they mentor

{{mentorsWithSchool}} mentor posts name a school — Elena Guriță to LT „Boris Dînga”, Criuleni, and so on. Nothing read it. The mentor found their school as one row among {{schools}}, and the detail page behind it showed only confirmed cycles, so a school midway through its first assessment showed an empty wheel and no progress. Supporting a school through the instrument is the entire job of this role.

Assignment.schoolId was written by the admin screen and read by no route

Fixed · was high

Training-need ranking read the wrong cycle

The strategic dashboard filtered on the official cycle and then read C1/C3/C4 from the current one. A school with a confirmed cycle 1 and a draft cycle 2 would have been ranked on unconfirmed figures. No school was in that state, so it was latent rather than visible — and the pilot reaches it the moment anyone starts a second cycle.

src/routes/strategic.js — r.cycle.ratings should have been r.officialCycle.ratings

Fixed · was medium

Missing ratings were scored as zero

An unrated C-indicator contributed 0 to the sort key, indistinguishable from a genuine level 0. A school that had simply not filled in Domain C appeared as the most urgent training case in the country.

src/routes/strategic.js — a sum over nullable levels

Fixed · was medium

Territorial flags went nowhere the flagger could see

Flagging wrote an audit entry and redirected. Reading it back needs admin.audit, which no territorial post holds. The Ministry was never notified either, so the button appeared to do nothing.

Fixed · was medium

No district filter on the national dashboard

The route queried the district list and passed it to the view; the view never used it, and filterRows had no territory branch. With {{districts}} raioane and a Ministry user whose commonest question is regional, this was the missing filter.

src/routes/ministry.js — territories was an unused local

Fixed · was low

view.school was unusable for anyone but a school team

The guard requires the SCHOOL_TEAM role as well as the capability, so the capability alone granted nothing. It was offered in the picker for every role and silently did nothing for six of the seven.

Fixed · was low

Validation records were read but never written

The Ministry detail page rendered a validation section no code path could populate. The external-validation workflow is on the roadmap; until then the heading only appears when there is something under it.

Fixed · was low

A school team with no school landed on the feedback page

homeFor fell through view.school when the post had no schoolId and settled on /feedback — technically reachable, but not an answer to “where am I supposed to start”.

The regression these fixes caused

Recorded because a document that only lists what was found, and never what the fixing broke, is a sales sheet rather than a record.

Fixed · was an outage

Every authenticated page returned 500 for anyone holding more than one post

The workspace switcher in the top bar read workspace.role without checking whether a post had been chosen. Before a choice there is none — which is the normal state on /workspace, the page that exists to make the choice.

Because the top bar sits in the layout, the throw happened while rendering the layout, so the error page could not render either and Express answered with a bare “Internal Server Error”. Every authenticated page failed at once.

Latent for as long as nobody held two posts. Giving the account that runs the pilot three posts made it reachable immediately, and login redirects straight to the picker, so it was the first page seen. Two tests now cover the unchosen state.
